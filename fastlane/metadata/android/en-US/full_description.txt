ZenMusic is a free, open-source music player built with Flutter. It brings several music sources into one clean, fast interface so you don't have to switch between apps.

<b>Features</b>
• Search and play from JioSaavn, YouTube Music and Spotify in one place
• Synced lyrics that follow the song
• Listen Together: sync playback with friends
• Offline downloads
• Song recognition
• Sleep timer and equalizer
• Customizable player skins, sliders and layouts

<b>Open source</b>
ZenMusic is licensed under GPL-3.0. Source code, issues and releases are on GitHub.

<b>Please note</b>
ZenMusic is still in active development, so you may run into bugs. Some sources may be unavailable in your region without a VPN or proxy. ZenMusic is an unofficial client and is not affiliated with YouTube, JioSaavn or Spotify.
