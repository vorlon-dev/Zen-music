## ZenMusic v25.11.2026

### New
- **Echo-style player screens** — Apple Music and Material 3 skins rebuilt after Echo Music: connected buttons, squircle transport, rotating cookie play button, spring-pressed controls, canvas support.
- **Player ⇄ lyrics transition** — one spring-settled motion: cover shrinks to a corner thumbnail, controls slide out, title row slides in, lyrics rise up. Drag the cover or title row to scrub it.
- **Echo mini player (phone)** — rounded pill with a progress ring around the artwork, cookie play button and swipe-to-skip.
- **YouTube canvas** — when Spotify has no canvas (or you are not signed in), the song's own music video plays as a muted 20-second loop, up to 1080p.
- **Player screen picker** — small preview cards for every style (drop screenshots in `assets/player`).
- **Lyric sources page redesign** — logo per source, word-by-word / line-by-line badges, drag to reorder, reset button (logos in `assets/lyrics`).
- **Settings redesign** — grouped tile cards, quick actions, real SVG brand logos (Spotify, YouTube Music, Last.fm, GitHub), working links, share and report-a-problem.
- **Appearance + Equalizer redesign** — live theme preview, segmented theme controls, accent swatches; equalizer with a live response curve, preset chips and a reset button.
- **Skeleton loading** — screens show placeholders until data arrives; first-load lag after YouTube Music / Spotify sign-in reduced.

### Fixed
- **Dynamic colors** now actually follow the playing song's artwork, and **Pure black background** now applies.
- **Back button** missing on Podcasts and other pages opened from the home chips and bottom sheets.
- **Black screen on app start** — the app now paints a logo screen immediately and the Android launch window shows the app icon.
- **Player sheet** — mini player and page crossfade over the same window (no empty dark frame while closing); snappier spring.
- **Lyrics button** was always disabled in the new skins.
- Settings rows: logos aligned to one size, dropdown rows no longer squeeze titles.
- Null-safety error in the synced-lyrics letter animation.

### Removed
- The experimental extension runtime.
